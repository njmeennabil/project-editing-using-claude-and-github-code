Every number and place on screen or in the narration, with where it comes from.

## Given by the person who commissioned the film
- Nabil's last exam day as a student at UPM Kampus Bintulu, Sarawak; flight on **18 November 2026**.
- Flight: Malaysia Airlines, Bintulu → KLIA Terminal 1. The flight number was given as "A1327"; Malaysia Airlines flight numbers start with MH, so the film shows **MH1327** (agreed in the brief). The number was not checked against a timetable.
- Home: his mother's house in **Ken Rimba, Shah Alam**. Then on to **Masjid An-Naim, Jeram**.
- Nabil is hardworking in business and is coming home to grow the family business.

## Distances
- **1,257 km**, Bintulu airport → KLIA: great-circle (haversine) distance between the two coordinates above, Earth radius 6,371.0088 km. Labelled "jarak lurus" (straight-line), not the flown distance.
- **51 km**, KLIA T1 → Ken Rimba: OSRM driving route over OpenStreetMap (router.project-osrm.org, 13 Nov 2026): 51.33 km, about 49 min. `data/osrm_klia_kenrimba.json`.
- **35 km**, Ken Rimba → Jeram: OSRM driving route, 34.53 km, about 27 min. `data/osrm_kenrimba_jeram.json`.
- **≈ 1,343 km** total: 1,257.0 + 51.33 + 34.53 = 1,342.9 km, rounded.
