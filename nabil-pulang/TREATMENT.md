# Nabil Pulang — treatment

Style: 3D Map Explainer. Language: Bahasa Melayu (voice, subtitles, labels). 1920×1080, 24 fps, ~54 s.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Funnel**: globe → Borneo → the flight → Selangor → the house → back out to the globe. Clear, but it is the style's cliché (and the library demo's shape).
2. **One journey along one line** (chosen): open close and low on Bintulu, climb with the aircraft, follow the line across the sea, hand it over from air to road at KLIA, end on the road to Jeram, then rise until the whole line from Bintulu to Jeram glows in one frame with the total held over it. The topic *is* a line across the country; the film should travel it.
3. **Two places compared**: Bintulu (student life) and Shah Alam/Jeram (family business) side by side on one scale. Good for the theme, but loses the sense of travelling home.

Chosen: **2**. Opening: a close, low shot over Bintulu that climbs (not a dive from orbit). Ending: a last quantity (total km) held over the whole route, with the closing line. Score shape: small and plucked at the start, moving (spiccato) over the sea, a breath of near-silence at the mother's house, then a warm horn swell for the "new chapter".

## Logline and arc

On the day of his last exam as a UPM Bintulu student, Nabil flies 1,257 km home across the South China Sea, drives to his mother's house in Shah Alam and on to Jeram, coming home not for a holiday but to grow the family business.

- **Setup**: Bintulu, last paper, last flight as a student.
- **Turn**: arrival at his mother's house in Ken Rimba (the breath); the journey continues.
- **Ending**: the whole line lit at once: from student to entrepreneur.

## Benchmark

- **Aircraft cabin moving-map displays**: learn the calm progress of one glyph along a great-circle arc, the distance read-out, the sense of time passing. Don't take their icons, colours or type.
- **News 3D terrain explainers**: learn the continuous camera from region to point, labels that sit on the ground and hand over with height. Don't take colours, camera paths or graphics packages.

## Titles and subtitles

- Barlow Condensed (OFL) for everything; Malay only, single line (DIRECTOR §1: a one-language film sets that line alone).
- Title: pin on UPM Bintulu, ring pulse, leader line to "NABIL PULANG" in the sky/sea space, date under it.
- Labels on the map: Sarawak, Laut China Selatan, Semenanjung Malaysia (far set); Bintulu, KLIA, Shah Alam, Jeram (near set); pins for UPM Bintulu, KLIA Terminal 1, Rumah ibunda · Ken Rimba, Masjid An-Naim · Jeram.
- Subtitles: map-legend shelf bottom-left, amber vertical rule, one Malay line, never wrapped.
- Sources in a corner for the whole film: terrain (AWS Terrain Tiles), imagery (Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX, CC BY 4.0), roads (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, via OSRM), flight path great-circle (schematic), vertical exaggeration.
