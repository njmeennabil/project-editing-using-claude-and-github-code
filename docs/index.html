    About

    How BUMI360 decides

    Each reading is compared with the crop's recommended range. The overall result follows the weakest reading.

    ReadingSuitableWith managementLess suitable
    Soil pHWithin range–Outside range
    TemperatureWithin optimumUp to 2°C outsideMore than 2°C outside
    WaterWithin rangeShort by up to half, or excessShort by more than half

    Water Spinach and Mustard Greens have no seasonal water figure in mm. For these crops, BUMI360 shows moisture advice and the water reading does not change the result.

    Data sources

    Crop requirements and fertiliser rates come from the BUMI360 crop database, with references including TNAU, Nepal guidelines and the Department of Agriculture Sarawak, as noted on each crop.

    Coming next

    Satellite rainfall (NASA GPM) and soil moisture (NASA SMAP) for Sabah and Sarawak, so your field readings can be filled in from your location.

    Please note

    BUMI360 gives general guidance. Confirm planting decisions with a soil test and your local agriculture officer.
